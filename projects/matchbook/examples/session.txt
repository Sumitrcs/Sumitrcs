# A short trading session. Sellers and buyers place orders that wait in the book.
sell 1 100 101.50
sell 2  50 101.50
sell 3 200 102.00
buy  4  80 101.00
buy  5  40 100.50
book

# A buyer willing to pay up to 101.75 takes the cheapest shares first.
# Order 1 arrived before order 2 at the same price, so it trades first.
buy 6 120 101.75

# A market sell takes the best bids, walking down the book.
sell 7 100 market

# Order 3 is cut to 150: it keeps its place. Order 5 (20 left) moves to a new price.
modify 3 150
modify 5 20 100.75

# Fill-or-kill: 500 can't be filled in full, so nothing trades.
buy 8 500 102.00 fok
# Post-only would trade against 101.50, so it is rejected.
buy 9 10 101.50 post
# Cancelling works once; after that the order is gone.
cancel 3
cancel 3
